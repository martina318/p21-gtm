A GTM system is made up of different layers that each do a different job.

GTM engineering connects those layers so information can move from raw data to context, from context to a decision, and from that decision into the right action.

Adding people scales execution. Engineering enhances decision-making.

Adding headcount increases how much a team can execute, but the same decisions still have to be made across every account: who to prioritize, when to act, which channel to use and where to spend.

GTM engineering puts that decision logic into the infrastructure, so it can run across every account continuously and improve based on what actually happens.
